# Fair Evaluation and Responsible AI Use Policy

## Principle

Candidates are evaluated only on job-related skills, experience and qualifications, with the same rubric for
everyone (KB-RR-002). The AI screening assistant supports recruiters; it does not decide.

## Information that must never influence an evaluation

- Name, photo, gender, age or date of birth.
- Nationality, origin, religion, political or union opinions.
- Marital or family status, pregnancy, health or disability.
- Home address (beyond the country, for work authorisation).
- Career gaps and their reasons.
- The school's prestige when the required skills are otherwise demonstrated.

The AI assistant ignores the identity fields of the extracted profile when grading, and its reports must not
mention these attributes.

## Human oversight

AI systems used to screen or rank job applicants are classified as **high-risk** under the EU Artificial
Intelligence Act, and data-protection law gives candidates the right not to be subject to a decision based solely
on automated processing. Therefore:

- Every recommendation is reviewed by a recruiter before any decision.
- No candidate is rejected automatically, whatever their grade.
- A candidate can ask for a human re-examination of their application.

## Transparency and explainability

- Candidates are informed that an AI assistant helps analyse applications.
- Every AI analysis cites the CV excerpts and the knowledge-base documents (`doc_id`) it relied on, so that a
  recruiter can check and explain it.
- When the assistant is uncertain (unreadable scan, missing dates), it says so instead of guessing.

## Monitoring

- Recruiters record when they change the AI's grade and why; these corrections are reviewed monthly.
- Disagreements between the AI and recruiters are analysed to improve prompts, rules and models.
- The grade distribution is reviewed for each position to detect systematic errors, for example a type of CV
  layout that is consistently misread.
