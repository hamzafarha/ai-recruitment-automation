# Remote Work and Flexible Hours Policy

## Eligibility

All technical roles (software engineering, ML/AI, data, DevOps) are eligible for remote or hybrid work. Experience
working in distributed teams is appreciated but is not a requirement and is never used to grade an application.

## Core working hours

Team members must be available for synchronous communication between 10:00 and 15:00 CET.

## Equipment and stipend

A home-office stipend of €1,500 is provided, together with a high-performance workstation.

## Location constraints

Remote employees must live in a country where the Company can legally employ them. Work authorisation is checked by
the recruiter during the process (see KB-RR-001).
