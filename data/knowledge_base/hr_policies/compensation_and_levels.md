# Engineering Career Ladder and Compensation Bands

## Levels and bands

| Level | Title | Relevant experience | Target base salary (€) |
|---|---|---|---|
| IC3 | Junior Engineer | 0–2 years | 45,000 – 60,000 |
| IC4 | Mid-Level Engineer | 2–5 years | 65,000 – 85,000 |
| IC5 | Senior Engineer | 5–8 years | 90,000 – 125,000 |
| IC6 | Staff Engineer / Tech Lead | 8+ years | 130,000 – 175,000 |

Stock options and performance bonuses (10–25 %) apply to levels IC4 and above.

## How the level is chosen

- The level is proposed from relevant experience counted with the rules in KB-RR-003, then confirmed by the
  interview panel.
- Salary expectations are discussed by the recruiter after the first interview. They are never used to grade
  the fit of an application.
- A candidate may be offered a different level from the one advertised when the interview shows a clear gap or a
  clear excess; the recruiter explains the decision to the candidate.
