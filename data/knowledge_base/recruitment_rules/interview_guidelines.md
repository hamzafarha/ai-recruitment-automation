# Structured Interview Guidelines

Structured interviews ask every candidate for the same role the same core questions and score answers on a common
scale. They are fairer and better at predicting job performance than unstructured conversations.

## Interview format

- **Duration**: 60 minutes: 5 min introduction, 40 min competency questions, 10 min candidate questions, 5 min
  closing.
- **Panel**: at least two interviewers, one of whom is from the hiring team.
- **Preparation**: interviewers read the screening analysis, especially the gaps marked "not evidenced", and choose
  questions that address them.
- Each interviewer scores independently before the panel discusses.

## Scoring scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 4 | Exceeds expectations: concrete, detailed examples with measurable impact |
| 3 | Meets expectations: relevant examples, sound reasoning |
| 2 | Partially meets: general answers, limited depth |
| 1 | Does not meet: no relevant example or incorrect reasoning |

## Turning screening gaps into questions

A gap marked "not evidenced" in the screening report is a question, not a verdict. Examples:

- *Requirement "Docker" listed but not demonstrated*: "Walk me through how you containerised a service: base image,
  build, and how it ran in production."
- *Experience 1 year below the requirement*: "Which project gave you the most ownership? What did you decide
  yourself?"
- *Equivalent skill (Azure instead of AWS)*: "How would you map what you built on Azure to AWS services?"

## Core question bank

- **Problem solving**: "Describe a hard bug or incident you resolved. How did you find the root cause?"
- **Collaboration**: "Tell me about a disagreement on a technical choice. How was it resolved?"
- **Ownership**: "Which result are you most proud of, and what was your personal contribution?"
- **Learning**: "What did you learn recently, and how did you apply it?"

Role-specific questions are listed in each job profile.

## Rules for interviewers

- Do not ask about age, family situation, religion, health, origin or any other protected attribute.
- Take notes on what the candidate said, not on impressions ("good vibe").
- Record scores and evidence in the recruitment system within 24 hours.
