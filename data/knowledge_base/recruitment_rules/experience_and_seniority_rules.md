# Experience Counting and Seniority Rules

Years of experience are one of the most common requirements and one of the most often miscounted. These rules make
the count consistent across recruiters and the AI assistant.

## Counting professional experience

- Count from the start date to the end date of each position. "Present" or "Current" means the screening date.
- **Overlapping positions are counted once**: two parallel jobs in 2020 count as one year, not two.
- When only years are given ("2018 – 2020"), count from July of the first year to June of the last year.
- When dates are missing or unreadable, do not guess: report the experience as "duration unknown".

## What counts, and how much

| Type of experience | Counted as |
|---|---|
| Full-time employment | 100 % |
| Part-time employment | 50 % |
| Freelance or contract work with identifiable deliverables | 100 % |
| Internship, apprenticeship, work-study | 50 %, at most 1 year in total |
| PhD or research position using the job's core skills | 50 % for ML, NLP and data roles; otherwise 0 % |
| Personal or open-source projects | Not counted as years, but used as evidence of skills |
| Teaching assistant or academic projects | Not counted as years |

## Relevant versus total experience

Job descriptions usually ask for **relevant** experience: years in positions that used the job's core skills. A
developer with 10 years of experience, of which 2 in Python, has 2 years of relevant Python experience. Always
report both numbers: "8 years total, 3 years relevant (Python backend)".

## Seniority levels

| Level | Relevant experience | Typical expectations |
|---|---|---|
| Junior (IC3) | 0–2 years | Delivers well-defined tasks with guidance |
| Mid-level (IC4) | 2–5 years | Owns features end to end, works autonomously |
| Senior (IC5) | 5–8 years | Designs systems, mentors others, leads technical choices |
| Staff / Tech Lead (IC6) | 8+ years | Sets technical direction across teams |

A job title alone ("Senior", "Lead") does not prove a level; the counted experience and the responsibilities
described in the CV do.

## Career gaps and changes

- Career gaps are **not** penalised and their reasons are never asked during screening.
- Career changers are assessed on transferable skills: a backend developer moving to ML keeps their software
  engineering experience, while their ML experience is counted from their first ML position or project.
